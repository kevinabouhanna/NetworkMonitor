# Phase 8: Your data outside the app

**Milestone:** Phase 8: Your data outside the app · **Status:** Later

**Goal:** NetworkMonitor's numbers where people already are: on the desktop, in Terminal, or
asked in plain words. All three are ideas: each starts by deciding who it's for and what it
should do, before anything is built.

## The plans

- **Widgets for today's data and speed** (#108): first, whether a widget can be built without
  Xcode, and whether minutes-old numbers are worth showing.
- **A `networkmonitor` command for Terminal** (#77): who it's for, which command first, and
  whether it only reads or can block too.
- **Ask an AI about what the network is doing** (#308): for Kevin with his own agent (an MCP
  server, the cheapest), or for every user (on-device, or a key of their own).
