# Phase 7: The launch

**Milestone:** Phase 7: The launch · **Status:** Later

**Goal:** the first public launch: the app at its best, shown where Mac users look, and
written up afterwards: what brought people, and what didn't.

## The plan

**Launch NetworkMonitor publicly** (#577), once the branding (#582) is done, in order:

1. polish the app as a first-time user would see it, on a fresh release (#573);
2. a table on the website comparing NetworkMonitor with Little Snitch, TripMode and AdGuard,
   with the legal side checked first (#596);
3. a launch video, Kevin's screen and face (#574);
4. posts on r/macapps, Hacker News and the other places that fit (#575);
5. a small paid ads experiment, after the posts settle (#576);
6. the Homebrew cask in Homebrew's own repository, once the app has about 75 stars (#529).
