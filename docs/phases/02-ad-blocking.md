# Phase 2: Ad blocking that holds up

**Milestone:** Phase 2: Ad blocking that holds up · **Status:** Next

**Goal:** the best ad blocking a Mac app can do in every app, with the controls people know
from other ad blockers.

## The plans

- **Find and check your own rules from the Connections window** (#592). A user couldn't
  find the rules they'd made: Connections' rules were listed only in its settings, and a
  rule's tag showed only while its row was in the list. Kevin approved the spec (13 Nov
  2026) and asked that ad blocking's rules work the same way: **Rules** beside Traffic and
  Blocked, and beside Overview and the Log, made of the same parts, each rule with what it
  did in the period, a menu to change it, Show and −; ad blocking's *Never block* and
  *Always block* become **Always Allow** and **Block**, Connections' words.
- **Ad blocking that holds up** (#246), what's left of it: **Enhanced ad blocking** (#256), an
  opt-in switch that removes ads inside pages and videos in every app, by opening secure
  connections on this Mac, never banking or health sites. Off by default.

Done (13 Nov 2026, not released yet): **pause ad blocking from the menu bar** (#600). The
popover's Ads blocked view opens with a bar saying whether ads are blocked, with **Pause** (for
an hour, until tomorrow, or on this network) or **Resume**, and the menu bar item's right-click
menu has the same.

The Chrome extension is a second product with its own store listing, so it's its own phase
(phase 3), as decided on 7 Nov 2026 (#256).
