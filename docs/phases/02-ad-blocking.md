# Phase 2: Ad blocking that holds up

**Milestone:** Phase 2: Ad blocking that holds up · **Status:** Now

**Goal:** the best ad blocking a Mac app can do in every app, with the controls people know
from other ad blockers.

## The plans

- **Pause ad blocking from the menu bar** (#600). Other ad blockers show a big pause button
  first, with the stats beside it or a click away. Kevin asked for the same.
- **Find and check your own rules from the Connections window** (#592). A user couldn't
  find the rules they'd made: Connections' rules are listed only in its settings, and a
  rule's tag shows only while its row is in the list. The spec (13 Nov 2026) recommends
  **Rules** beside Traffic and Blocked, listing every rule with what it did in the period,
  and the same in Ads and Trackers; building it waits for Kevin's pick.
- **Ad blocking that holds up** (#246), what's left of it: **Enhanced ad blocking** (#256), an
  opt-in switch that removes ads inside pages and videos in every app, by opening secure
  connections on this Mac, never banking or health sites. Off by default.

The Chrome extension is a second product with its own store listing, so it's its own phase
(phase 3), as decided on 7 Nov 2026 (#256).
