- **Updates have a place of their own in Settings**, near the top: your version, when it last checked, and a sentence that says what will happen (#710).
- **Install updates automatically.** A new switch, off to begin with, in Settings and the welcome window. New versions install by themselves while NetworkMonitor's windows are closed, never during a speed test or on a hotspot, and a card tells you afterwards (#710).
- **The window you had open comes back** after an update (#710).
- **The update card no longer makes the window taller**, and says a version is available rather than ready when it hasn't downloaded (#710).
- **YouTube plays without its ads** when Enhanced ad blocking is on, and its pages load faster with it on than they did (#741).
- **Pages in Safari, Mail and other apps' web views count for the app**, no longer for System, and a rule on the app covers them (#761, with defragekb).
- **Your own network's traffic no longer counts as internet** in apps like remote desktops, whose connections don't say where they go, also right after you change networks. It needs Hotspot mode's helper (#753, #759, with defragekb).
