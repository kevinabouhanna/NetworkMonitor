- **In the Dock, the menu bar, or both.** Settings › Show NetworkMonitor in puts it in the menu bar, the Dock or both; Settings' *Menu bar colors* is now *Speed colors* (#69, #517).
- **显示在程序坞、菜单栏或两者中。** 在“设置 › NetworkMonitor 显示在”中，可以选择菜单栏、程序坞或两处；“菜单栏颜色”现在叫“网速颜色”（#69、#517）。
- **The Dock icon can be your speeds.** Under Show NetworkMonitor in › Dock or Both, Dock icon: Speeds makes the whole icon the live speeds, drawn like the app's icon in light or dark, and App icon keeps the normal icon; Dock starts with Speeds, Both with App icon (#69, #523).
- **程序坞图标可以显示网速。** 在“NetworkMonitor 显示在 › 程序坞”或“两者”下，程序坞图标选择“网速”时，整个图标显示实时网速，画法与应用图标相同，随浅色或深色模式变化；选择“应用图标”则保留原来的图标。程序坞一开始为网速，两者一开始为应用图标（#69、#523）。
- **List, Both or Chart.** Usage History and Ads and Trackers show the list, the list and chart side by side as Connections has its list and map, or the chart alone; Ads and Trackers shows its Sites or Apps one at a time (#524, #534).
- **列表、两者或图表。** 流量历史和广告与跟踪器可以只显示列表、像“连接”的列表和地图一样并排显示列表和图表，或只显示图表；广告与跟踪器的网站和应用列表改为一次显示一个（#524、#534）。
- **Ads and Trackers' list and chart work like Usage History's.** Hover or click a bar to narrow the list to that hour or day, choose a site or app to see its share of each bar, open it to see the apps or sites behind it and when each was last blocked, and right-click or use the arrow keys, Space and Escape (#536).
- **广告与跟踪器的列表和图表用法与流量历史相同。** 指向或点按一根柱，列表就只显示那一小时或那一天；选中一个网站或应用，即可看到它在每根柱中所占的部分；展开它，可以看到背后的应用或网站以及各自最近一次拦截的时间；还可以右键点按，或使用方向键、空格键和 Esc 键（#536）。
- **One total in Ads and Trackers.** The header shows just how many were blocked; the ⓘ beside it lists what each list blocked, and marks a list that's switched off since (#402).
- **“广告与跟踪器”只显示一个总数。** 标题只显示拦截了多少次；旁边的 ⓘ 列出各列表的拦截次数，并标出之后已关闭的列表（#402）。
- **Names come first.** Beside a chart or the map, a row's bar shortens before its name does, and tags such as ADS or ALWAYS ALLOWED become icons before a name is cut short (#534).
- **名称优先。** 在图表或地图旁，一行的条形会先变短，名称保持完整；“广告”“始终允许”等标签会先变成图标，然后名称才会被截短（#534）。
- **The Connections map starts from where your Mac really is.** It uses Location when you've already allowed it for Wi-Fi names, then your Mac's own internet address, then its Region setting, and hovering the starting point says which (#231).
- **“连接”地图从你的 Mac 真正所在的地方开始。** 如果你已经为 Wi-Fi 名称允许了定位服务，就使用定位服务，其次是 Mac 自己的互联网地址，再次是“地区”设置；把指针悬停在起点上可以看到用的是哪一个（#231）。
- **Tab shows each ⓘ.** With Keyboard navigation on, an ⓘ now appears with its focus ring when Tab reaches it, rather than taking focus while invisible (#512).
- **按 Tab 键时显示每个 ⓘ。** 打开“键盘导航”后，按 Tab 键移到 ⓘ 时，它会连同焦点环一起显示，不会再在看不见的情况下获得焦点（#512）。
