- **Trackers behind a site's own name are caught for longer.** NetworkMonitor now remembers what a site's name stands for as long as the lookup said, up to a day, reads lookups answered over TCP too, and your rules apply to those names: *Always block* on a tracker also stops a site's own name for it, and *Never block* in one app covers them in that app (#321).
- **An app's rules follow its signature.** *Don't filter* and your other rules for one app hold only for the app signed by the developer it was made for, as Connections' rules do, so a different program put in its place doesn't inherit them (#320).
- **Extra lists read more faithfully.** In downloaded lists, a block marked `$important` now wins over the list's exceptions, and `$badfilter` cancels only the rule it names (#322).
- **Safer weekly lists.** The weekly update refuses a list that lost more than half its sites since the week before, so a broken source can't quietly turn blocking off (#319).
