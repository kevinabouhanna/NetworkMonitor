- **One window, with a sidebar.** Usage History, Connections, Ads and Trackers and Network are sections of one window, with Settings at the bottom of the sidebar. Each section's gear shows its own settings in place, with a back button (#436).
- **全新的单一窗口，带边栏。** 流量历史、连接、广告与跟踪器和网络现在是同一个窗口中的各部分，设置位于边栏底部。点按各部分的齿轮图标，即可在原处查看该部分的设置，并可返回（#436）。
- **A popover for today, this week or this month.** Data, Connections, Ads blocked and Network, each a click away, with lists you can open to see what's behind a company, a site or an app (#436, #449).
- **菜单栏弹出窗口可查看今天、本周或本月。** 数据、连接、已拦截和网络一点即达，列表中的每一项都可以展开，查看公司、网站或应用背后的详情（#436、#449）。
- **"This is a hotspot", on every network.** Mark a phone's hotspot that macOS doesn't recognise, from the popover or the Network section (#436).
- **在任何网络上都可以设为“这是热点”。** 如果 macOS 没有识别出手机热点，可以在弹出窗口或“网络”部分中手动标记（#436）。
- **One look, one set of colours.** Data is blue, connections teal, what's blocked red and hotspots orange, everywhere, with search in every section's toolbar (⌘F) and smoother movement (#436).
- **统一的外观和配色。** 数据为蓝色、连接为青色、已拦截为红色、热点为橙色，各处一致；每个部分的工具栏都有搜索（⌘F），动画也更流畅（#436）。
- **Settings no longer flashes** when it's opened again from the menu bar (#399).
- **从菜单栏再次打开设置时不再闪烁**（#399）。
- **Apache 2.0 licence.** NetworkMonitor is now free and open source under the Apache 2.0 licence (#444).
- **采用 Apache 2.0 许可证。** NetworkMonitor 现以 Apache 2.0 许可证免费开源（#444）。
