- **Ask Me names the site.** The panel says which site an app is reaching, Chrome included, and you can answer for that site or the whole company, and allow it once, until the app quits, or always (#199).
- **Block something on one network only.** Right-click in Connections to block it on the network you're on, like the office Wi-Fi, and nowhere else (#200).
- **Export and import your rules.** Move them to another Mac, or keep a backup, from Settings › Connections (#201).
- **Rules follow the app's signature.** A rule about an app no longer applies to a program someone else put in its place (#202).
