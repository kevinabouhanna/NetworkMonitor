- **A test switch for Safari with iCloud Private Relay.** It's off unless turned on by hand, and it's there to find out why Safari's downloads go missing from its row with Private Relay on. When it's on, it only writes what macOS counts for each connection to the content filter's log (#786, with defragekb).
- **Live, at the top of the popover.** The speeds right now, in your speed colors, beside ⊞ and the gear; click it for a graph over 1, 5 or 15 minutes, the apps using the network now with their speeds, and the speed test, which moves here from Network. The popover opens on it (#802, asked for by defragekb; #812).
- **Live in the main window.** First in the sidebar (⌘L): the speeds now, a wide graph, every app using the network and the speed test (#812).
- **Today, Week and Month are in the views they change**: Data, Connections and Ads blocked, no longer over Live and Network (#812).
- **Choose what the popover shows.** **Settings › Popover** shows or hides each view and puts Data, Connections, Ads blocked and Network in your order (#802).
- **Your addresses in Network.** This Mac's address on the network, and once you turn it on, the address websites see, with its country and provider (#803).
- **Updates come once you leave a hotspot.** A daily check skipped on a hotspot now happens as soon as you are back on an ordinary connection, instead of a day later (#810).
- **Live and Settings fill the window.** On a wide window, Live's apps fill the left and scroll by themselves, and the speed test has a column of its own on the right with your last result, so it no longer moves as apps come and go. Settings, Network and each section's settings run across the whole window (#825).
- **The Connections map tells amounts apart.** Each country is shaded in one of six steps, from under 1 MB to 10 GB or more, with a key in the map's corner, so a country with gigabytes no longer looks like one with megabytes. Pointing at a country still shows its real amount (#797).
- **Connections: the list alone in List, and a map or a chart beside it.** List no longer has a chart above it, as in the other sections. In Both and Map, **Map | Chart** in the pane's corner shows the map or the period's traffic as a chart, where you click or drag to see only that time; your choice is remembered (#798).
- **Filters say what they are, with Show everything.** In Connections, Usage History and Ads and Trackers, a line under the switches reads "Showing only …", each part with its own ✕, and **Show everything** clears them all at once, in place of the small chips people missed (#798).
- **Light, Dark or System for the Dock's speeds.** With **Dock icon** set to **Speeds**, **Settings › Appearance** keeps the Dock icon light or dark whatever your Mac is set to, or, with **System**, follows the Mac as before. It changes at once, even when both speeds are zero (#807, asked for by defragekb).
