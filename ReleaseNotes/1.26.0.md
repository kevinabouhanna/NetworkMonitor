- **Recommended ad lists.** NetworkMonitor now picks the extra lists for you: HaGeZi Normal and OISD Big, which between them block most ads and rarely break a site. Every other list, and adding one by its address, is under Advanced in the Lists tab; lists you'd already chosen stay as they were (#314).
- **No more hidden toolbar controls.** Ads and Trackers and Connections can no longer be narrowed past the width their toolbars need, so nothing folds into a » menu (#315).
- **Allowing an app no longer lets its ads through.** Always Allow on an app, a company or a country still lets it connect, but the ad and tracker lists now have their say on it too; allowing a single site still lets that site through (#316).
- **Sites named the way the web names them.** NetworkMonitor now uses the Public Suffix List to tell where a site's name ends, so addresses like moph.gov.lb or a shop on myshopify.com count as sites of their own in Connections, the Ads window and Ask Me (#318).
