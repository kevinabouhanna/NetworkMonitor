- **Block ads, trackers and malware in every app.** One switch in Settings › Connections blocks the sites on two lists, StevenBlack's hosts and URLhaus, Chrome included. Your own rules come first, and the popover says how many it blocked today (#176).
- **Block Everything.** One item in the menu bar's right-click menu stops every app's connections until you turn it off; the readout turns red while it's on (#198).
- **Connections names what Chrome reaches.** Apps that connect by address now get the site's name from the connection's handshake, so far fewer servers read *Hosted on Amazon* (#196).
- **Block or allow a whole site by name.** Right-click a server with a name for the name itself or *All of \*.site* (#197).
- **DNS servers keep their own names.** 1.1.1.1 no longer shows whatever your Mac last looked up (#196).
