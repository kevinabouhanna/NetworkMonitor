- **Your own ad-blocking rules.** A Rules tab in Ads and Trackers: never block a site, always block one no list has, in every app or just one, or leave an app's connections alone entirely. Right-click a site or app in the Overview or the Log to make one (#253).
- **Bring your rules over from uBlock Origin or AdGuard.** Import a uBlock Origin backup, AdGuard's exported settings, or rules you paste, see what comes over before anything changes, and export yours in the same format (#253).
- **Turn ad blocking off on a network**, such as one at work that needs a listed site, from Pause in Ads and Trackers (#253).
- **A filter that goes quiet is noticed and brought back.** After an update, macOS once held every new connection for half an hour without handing them to the filter. NetworkMonitor now tests a filter it hasn't heard from, reconnects, and restarts it if needed (#272).
